02What you get

◆ Encoding pipeline

Chain 52+ transforms — Base64, hex, URL, Unicode, ROT13, Morse, Braille, XOR — and watch each step live.

◆ Proxy suite

Burp-like repeater, intruder with marker-based fuzzing, decoder chain and sequencer. No license dialog.

◆ Rev shell generator

One-liners for 20+ languages and platforms, with encoding variants and a listener command builder.

◆ Crypto toolkit

Hashing, XOR brute-force, known-plaintext attacks, frequency analysis, Vigenère and classical ciphers.

◆ Offline intel

NVD CVE search, an offline Exploit-DB browser, CVSS calculator and a searchable vuln database.

◆ Flipper Zero library

Reference guide and payload library for your favorite dolphin. Pairs nicely with flipper-portals.

04Why it exists

The decisions worth knowing about, written down.

Offline-first means engagement-proof

Client networks are locked down. CTF boxes have no internet. SlimeShell doesn't care — everything runs client-side in a Tauri shell.

No telemetry, ever

A security tool that phones home is a contradiction. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no account system.

Built by someone who plays CTFs

The payload library, the cheatsheets, the Flipper section — they exist because they were needed at 1 AM mid-challenge.
